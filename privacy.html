Privacy policy

Rocket Rover for iPhone (Android to follow) · Last updated 5 November 2026

Rocket Rover does not collect data about you. There is no account, no login and no server of ours. Your garage, records and trophies are saved as small files on your own phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple (or Google Play on Android).

Who we are

Rocket Rover is made and published by Vibalyze OÜ. You can reach us at mert@maindtec.de.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for the game, so there is nowhere for us to receive data. The app has no account or sign-in, no analytics service, no crash reporting service, no Game Center or other leaderboard, and no social integrations. It does not ask for your email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

The only outside party involved is Apple (or Google Play on Android), for purchases and the rating prompt. Both are covered below.

What the app stores on your device

A handful of small JSON files in the app’s own documents folder:

  • career.json — your current career: your car, parts, cash and progress.
  • records.json — your personal records and Grand Prix results.
  • legacy.json — your hall and trophies, kept across careers.
  • settings.json — your Challenge level and whether haptics and sound are on.
  • meta.json — the cosmetics you own and your season track progress.

None of these files contains your name, contact details or a device identifier, and none of them is sent anywhere by us.

Device backups

Because the saves live in the app’s documents folder, they are included in your phone’s normal backup (device or iCloud backup on iOS, Android’s own backup on Android). Those backups are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies and your own backup settings. We cannot see them.

No ads, no tracking

Rocket Rover has no ads. The app contains no ad SDK, no analytics and no tracking of any kind, and it never reads your device’s advertising identifier.

Purchases

Rocket Rover sells a few one-time purchases: Rocket Rover Pro, the Season Pass (season 1, cosmetic) and cosmetic packs. No coins, currency or race advantages are sold. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit), or by Google Play Billing on Android. We never see your card or payment details; the app only receives a receipt saying which product you own. Restore purchases is available in Settings and in the Store.

The rating prompt

The game may occasionally ask the operating system to show its standard “rate this app” sheet (StoreKit’s review request on iOS). That sheet is drawn and run by Apple or Google, not by us; any rating or review you leave goes to the store under its own privacy policy.

Haptics and sound

Haptics only use your phone’s vibration engine. Both haptics and sound can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Progress lives only on your device. Uninstalling Rocket Rover deletes the app’s documents folder and every save file with it; there is nothing left on a server of ours, because there is none. Start over in Settings ends the current career. Your phone’s backup may still hold a copy after you uninstall; that is cleared in your device’s backup settings. Purchases stay tied to your Apple or Google account and can be restored.

Children

Rocket Rover is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect personal information at all.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the GDPR, gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. For purchases, contact Apple (or Google Play on Android).

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe — ads, analytics, accounts, cloud save, online play — this page is updated first, with a new date at the top, and the store privacy answers are updated with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything on this page: mert@maindtec.de.
