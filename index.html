ROCKET ROVER

Lean. Boost. Beat them round.

What it is

Race a rival round a tiny planet. The throttle is automatic: you hold left to lean back, hold right to lean forward and tap boost, while your rival comes at you head-on from the far side. Win races, earn cash, bolt new parts onto your rover, and take on tougher rivals and the Weekly Grand Prix.

  • Two thumbs
  • Tiny planets
  • Head-on rivals
  • Build your rover
  • Grand Prix

Fair play

No coins or race advantages for sale. No ads. No account needed; your progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
