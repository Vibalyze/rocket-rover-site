How to play

  • The throttle is automatic. Your rover drives itself; you handle the balance.
  • Hold the left side of the screen to lean back.
  • Hold the right side to lean forward.
  • Tap boost for a burst of speed.
  • Race to the far side of a tiny planet. Your rival starts on the other side and comes at you head-on. First one there wins.

The game plays in landscape.

FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore in the Store. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Rocket Rover Pro, the Season Pass and cosmetic packs are one-time purchases and always restorable.

Are there ads?

No. Rocket Rover has no ads.

What does Rocket Rover Pro give?

A 2× prize button after wins, free repairs, contract rerolls, an extra weekly Grand Prix attempt (capped per day), a 2× daily crate and an exclusive Pro paint.

What is Second Chance?

The first time you go broke with no way back onto a street, the tow truck offers a scrap car and a little cash to get you going again. It comes once per career, for free.

How do I start over?

Settings → Start over. It ends the current career. Your trophies and hall stay, and so do your purchases.

It is too hard / too easy

Change the Challenge level in Settings: Chill, Normal or Hard.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device only — there is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Your phone’s normal backup includes it, so restoring a device backup brings it back. We have no copy and cannot restore it for you. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue them ourselves, but we would like to know what went wrong.
